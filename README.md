## 📋 Problem Statement

GEDA Mission LiFE movement ke through nagrikon ko sustainable daily habits apnanee ke liye prerit kar raha hai. Lekin:

- **Awareness high hai, action low** — log climate change samajhte hain par daily behaviour nahi badalte
- **Intent-action gap** — rooftop solar lagwane ka mann hota hai, par feasibility, subsidy, aur vendor discovery ka koi personalised tool nahi
- **No feedback loop** — generic carbon apps sirf number dikhate hain
- **Community untapped** — individual efforts ko momentum mein badalne ka mechanism nahi

---

## 💡 Solution

SolarSetu ek **Progressive Web App (PWA)** hai jo Mission LiFE ke chaar core pillars ko operationalise karta hai:

### 1. 📊 Personalised Footprint Tracker
Pin code aur bill enter karte hi baseline carbon footprint calculate hota hai, saath hi rooftop solar feasibility score, subsidy breakdown, aur 25-year savings projection dikhta hai.

### 2. 📸 SolarLens — AR Rooftop Scanner
Rooftop photo upload karo, AR overlay se panel placement visualize hota hai. Roof area, panel count, capacity, aur monthly/yearly savings instant calculate.

### 3. 🧾 AI Bill Scanner
Electricity bill upload karo — AI engine energy breakdown (AC, lighting, appliances), waste patterns, aur personalised savings plan deta hai.

### 4. 🤝 Community & Solar Saathi
Neighbourhood leaderboard par compete karo, aur group solar pools bana kar 12-18% extra vendor discount unlock karo.

### 5. 🏆 Rewards & Gamification
Daily green missions complete karo, streaks maintain karo, Green Coins kamayein, aur real rewards redeem karein (chai discount, sapling, solar coupons).

### 6. 📞 GEDA-Verified Vendor Directory
12 empanelled vendors, district filters, ratings, services — direct callback request with phone validation.
